# Release Notes — 0.1.1 (3)

ClipVault 0.1.1 makes large clip libraries faster and the reading workspace clearer.

- Clip selection now uses cached workspace sections and indexed lookup instead of regrouping the entire visible library on every click.
- The clip list adds readable titles, bounded previews, type-aware metadata, and stable date grouping for large libraries.
- The detail and AI surfaces are combined into one adaptable workspace with a full-window focus mode.
- AI results stay inline and can be dismissed without disturbing the open clip.
- Custom AI controls add brief pointer press feedback while respecting Reduce Motion; keyboard activation remains still.

Clipboard storage, capture consent, encryption, and local Foundation Models behavior are unchanged.
